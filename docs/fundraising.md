# Fundraising

## Giving experience

- State the cause, the local work, and why support matters in plain language.
- Present frequency, amount, PayPal/card, and bank transfer as one clear giving experience. Show only configured payment routes and explain any PayPal handoff.
- Keep monthly giving visible without preselecting it. Make one-time giving equally clear.
- Place verified care uses and transparency information near the giving choices.
- Offer approximate MAD equivalents only with an updateable exchange rate. Do not promise an exact outcome per gift until local costs are confirmed, or request fee coverage until actual processing fees are verified.
- Show bank details only when configured and make copying them easy on phones.

## Trust and donor care

- Use only verified care uses and transparency claims. Do not imply that reports or receipts are public unless they are actually published.
- Be clear about what the site and payment provider handle. Never imply a payment succeeded without confirmation.
- Thank donors promptly and honestly. Share real progress over time. Make future updates optional and respect consent.
- Measure completed gifts through provider records where available; a Donate click is only intent.

## Sources

- [Nielsen Norman Group](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/donation-usability/) — donors need clear mission and use-of-funds information; usability findings are qualitative, not a current conversion benchmark.
- [M+R Benchmarks 2025](https://2025.mrbenchmarks.com/key-findings.html) — examples of common one-time and monthly giving options; use benchmarks as context, not promises.
- [Association of Fundraising Professionals](https://afpglobal.org/news/why-we-say-thank-you) — gratitude and impact updates are ongoing stewardship.
