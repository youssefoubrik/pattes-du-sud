1. A closer look at Mirleft

A beautiful place — and many animals in need.

Mirleft is a paradise on Morocco’s Atlantic coast.
However, many dogs and cats live in the streets — without protection, without medical care, and without safety.

- Hunger and lack of water
- Injuries and illnesses
- Parasites
- Uncontrolled reproduction
- Road accidents
- Abuse and neglect
- Harsh living conditions

They are part of our community.
And they need our support.

2. Our Vision

Sustainable protection for animals in Mirleft.

We are not satisfied with simply rescuing animals;
we want to improve their situation in the long term
by helping them live better lives, with dignity, respect and well-being.

Our objectives

- Medical care and veterinary follow-up
- Vaccinations and treatments
- Sterilisation and neutering
- Adequate food and safe shelter
- Care for injured and sick animals
- Adoption and rehoming
- Monitoring and documentation
- Awareness and education

Together for a better future
for the animals of Mirleft.

3. Why spaying and neutering?

A single cat or a single dog can have many offspring during its lifetime.

This is why we act:

Help → Neuter → Vaccinate → Monitor

In this way, we prevent new suffering and provide better living conditions for all animals in Mirleft.

Fewer uncontrolled animals
=
Less suffering.


PAGE 6

4. Our system for stray dogs

The red collar = Protection & Safety

- The dog is identified and documented.
- It has been vaccinated.
- It has been spayed/neutered.
- It is observed and regularly monitored as much as possible.

PLEASE DO NOT REMOVE IT!

This collar helps to identify animals that have already been taken care of and prevents unnecessary or repeated interventions.

8. Who are we?

Southern Paws Association

Because every life matters.

- Fewer dangerous situations
- A more caring environment

- More collaboration

Our goal: a better future
